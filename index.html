ABOUT THE EVENT

WHERE CAMPUS IDEAS BECOME VENTURES

VentureVerse 2026 is the flagship entrepreneurship event of the Entrepreneurship Development Cell at JSS University, Noida. In one energetic day, students meet working founders, test their ideas in a live sprint, learn how to pitch with confidence, and connect with a community that backs builders.

Whether you're carrying a half-formed idea, a prototype, or just curiosity — there's a seat for you.
